What this is

POAP - Proof of Attendance Protocol - issued badges for events from 2018 until it shut down on 3 August 2026. The tokens themselves live on Gnosis Chain and are permanent. What they pointed at - the names, dates, descriptions and artwork - was hosted by POAP, and that hosting ended with the company.

In POAP's final days every event's metadata and artwork was crawled from its own hosts and verified byte-for-byte against the SHA-256 of each file as served. That is what this site shows: 197,577 events, 181,554 unique artworks, 145 MB of metadata and 194 GB of images. Nothing has been edited. Where an organiser typed the year as 3463, it still says 3463.

This site is not affiliated with POAP. It exists because the archive is only useful if people can find things in it.

If you are here to save your own badges rather than browse everyone's, use poap-saver - enter your address and it writes your badges to your disk.

With kubo installed and ipfs daemon running, ipfs pin add <root> fetches and keeps a copy. Pinning the metadata root is the single most useful thing you can do: it is 145 MB and it is the part that cannot be rebuilt from anything else. A hash proves a file is unaltered, but it will not tell you what the event was called.

The full registry - every event id, its artwork hash and content type - is in mdws-org/poap-saver. This site's own source is mdws-org/everybadge.
